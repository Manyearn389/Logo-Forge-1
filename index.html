Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy outlines how LogoForge AI collects, protects, and manages your personal information and logo creations.

1 Overview & Commitment

LogoForge AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides a modern, AI-powered vector logo creation and brand identity platform. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your branding assets, personal data, and account security.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, register an account, utilize our vector synthesis engine, and export branding packages.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information only as necessary to provide, optimize, and secure our logo generation services:

  • Logo Generation Inputs: Business names, slogans, chosen industry categories, color scheme preferences, style selections, and custom prompt keywords you provide to synthesize vector marks.
  • Custom Uploaded Reference Images: If you upload reference imagery, graphics, or sketches, they are processed within your client browser session strictly to assist vector synthesis and are not sold, distributed, or used for unauthorized third-party training.
  • User Account Details: When you create an account, we collect your display name and email address.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: IP address, device type, browser platform, operating system, and performance telemetry to detect errors and improve platform performance.

3 Firebase Authentication & Cloud Storage

LogoForge AI utilizes Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore for user authentication and cloud synchronization:

  • Secure Sign-In: Your email and password credentials are managed through Google Firebase's industry-standard cryptographic infrastructure. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Authentication State: Firebase securely issues cryptographic auth tokens to verify your login session across devices.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When authenticated, your saved logos, color palettes, and brand configurations are securely persisted to your private Firestore collection so you can access them across visits.

4 Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser storage technologies to provide an optimal and uninterrupted user experience:

  • Essential Session Cookies: Used by Firebase Authentication to keep you securely signed in as you navigate between the generator, gallery, and account dashboards.
  • Local Storage (localStorage): Stores your UI theme preference (Dark or Light Mode), temporary generator drafts, and onboarding splash screen state directly in your browser.
  • You can manage or clear your browser cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings.

5 Advertising, Google AdSense & Google AdMob

LogoForge AI integrates advertising services, including Google AdSense and Google AdMob:

  • Ad Delivery: We display standard banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad units to support free logo generation tools for all users.
  • Google Cookies & Web Beacons: Google and its advertising partners use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on visits to this and other websites.
  • Google AdMob & Partner Delivery: Advertising units are delivered using Google AdMob and Google AdSense guidelines, ensuring compliance with COPPA and global privacy standards without storing unencrypted user identity markers.
  • Opt-Out Options: You may opt out of personalized Google advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

6 Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with reputable, privacy-conscious service infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase & Google Cloud: For identity verification, cloud database records, and server hosting.
  • Google Fonts: For serving typography styles used within vector designs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or logo creations to third-party data brokers under any circumstances.

7 User-Generated Logos & Intellectual Property

Full Ownership: You retain full commercial ownership and intellectual property rights to the logos and branding assets you generate and export using LogoForge AI.

Our procedural vector synthesis engine generates mathematically defined SVG code. Once downloaded, you have the full right to trademark, modify, print, publish, and commercially distribute your logo across digital and physical media.

8 Account Deletion & Data Rights

We believe in complete data autonomy. You can permanently delete your LogoForge AI account and all associated data at any time:

  • Navigate to your Account page from the top menu or bottom bar.
  • Click the red "Delete Account" button.
  • Confirm the prompt. Your user record, authentication credentials, and all saved cloud logos will be permanently and irreversibly purged from our database immediately.

9 Contact & Data Protection

If you have questions, inquiries, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact our Data Protection team at:

LogoForge AI is an AI-powered logo creation platform built to democratize world-class brand design. We believe every entrepreneur, creator, startup, and small business deserves an iconic, professional brand identity without paying thousands of dollars to design agencies or waiting weeks for initial sketches.

By merging modern generative aesthetic principles with strict mathematical vector rendering, LogoForge AI synthesizes production-ready brand marks in fractions of a second.

1 What Makes LogoForge AI Different?

Most generic AI tools output fuzzy raster images (JPEG/PNG) that turn blurry when enlarged. LogoForge AI is fundamentally different:

  • True Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG): Our vector engine outputs mathematically defined XML paths. Your logo will look just as razor-sharp on a 50-foot billboard as it does on a favicon.
  • 4 Simultaneous Style Variations: Every generation produces four distinct aesthetic archetypes: Modern Minimalist, Emblem & Crest, Abstract Geometric, and Dynamic Tech / Typographic.
  • Client-Side Engine Speed: Experience instant responsiveness, real-time color adjustments, and immediate vector exports with zero latency.
  • High-Resolution Raster Exports: Export up to 8192 × 8192 px master PNGs with crystal-clear alpha transparency and web-ready JPEGs.

2 Complete Brand Experience

A logo does not exist in a vacuum. LogoForge AI includes interactive, real-world mockups so you can see your brand in action before exporting:

  • iOS & Android App Icon: Preview squircle launcher icons with gradient depths and specular lighting.
  • Executive Business Card: Inspect your logo on matte luxury cardstock with gold foil debossing.
  • Website Hero & Header: Test how your typography and brand mark integrate seamlessly into modern web navigation.
